About

About KubeOpsAI

We build Kubernetes observability tooling for AI assistants.

Engineering teams spend too much time switching between dashboards, running manual queries, and piecing together context during incidents. We built K8s Telemetry MCP Server to eliminate that friction — letting your AI assistant do the querying while you focus on fixing the problem.

Our product implements the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard that allows AI assistants like Amazon Q, Claude, and Bedrock agents to call external tools and retrieve structured data. Every tool is read-only and every response is sanitized before it reaches your AI.
