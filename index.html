🔒 Security & Privacy

Your telemetry never reaches us.
That's not a policy. It's the architecture.

Every other incident response tool sends your logs, metrics, and traces to their SaaS platform. KubeOpsAI has no control plane and no SaaS backend: the agent runs inside your cluster and we never receive your data. It leaves your VPC in exactly two places you choose — the report posted to your Slack, and the prompt sent to your own LLM provider. Pick Bedrock and it never leaves AWS.

🏠

Runs in your cluster

The agent is a pod inside your EKS cluster. It queries your observability stack directly over the cluster network, so collection never crosses your VPC boundary and needs no outbound path to us.

👁️

Read-only by design

No tool can create, modify or delete an AWS or Kubernetes resource — those operations do not exist in the codebase. The agent investigates and reports; it writes only where you point it, to Slack and your ticketing system.

🔑

Bring your own LLM key

You provide your own Bedrock, Anthropic, or OpenAI key. We never see your API keys, your logs, your metrics, or your traces.

🧹

Secrets scrubbed

AWS keys, JWTs, bearer tokens, password= assignments, and database URIs are redacted before data reaches the LLM. Defence in depth, documented in full.

🌐

No ingress required

No load balancers, no exposed ports, no public endpoints. The MCP server communicates over stdio via kubectl exec.

📋

Scoped RBAC

A minimal ClusterRole grants get and list on events, nodes, pods, deployments, replicasets and HPAs — and nothing else. No secrets, no exec, no write verbs, no wildcards.
