Root Cause
checkout-api was OOMKilled due to an unbounded in-memory cache introduced in v2.4.1, deployed 14 minutes before the alert fired. Memory spiked from 180Mi to 512Mi limit.
Evidence
• K8s event: OOMKilled × 3 in 10 min (03:04–03:14 UTC)

• Recent deploy: checkout-api v2.4.1 at 03:00:11 UTC
• Log pattern: "cache.put() called 12,000×/min" (new in v2.4.1)
Recommended Actions
1. Roll back checkout-api to v2.4.0 immediately
2. Add cache size limit (max 10,000 entries)
3. Raise memory limit to 768Mi as short-term mitigation

🔒 Security & Privacy

Your data never leaves your VPC.
That's not a feature. It's the architecture.

Every other incident response tool sends your logs, metrics, and traces to their SaaS platform. KubeOpsAI runs entirely inside your cluster. Your data stays in your infrastructure — always.

🏠

Runs in your cluster

The agent is a pod inside your EKS cluster. It queries your observability stack directly over the cluster network. Nothing leaves your VPC.

👁️

Read-only by design

All tools are strictly read-only. No write, delete, or mutation operations exist in the codebase. The agent investigates — it cannot change anything.

🔑

Bring your own LLM key

You provide your own Bedrock, Anthropic, or OpenAI key. We never see your API keys, your logs, your metrics, or your traces.

🧹

PII auto-redacted

Secrets, tokens, AWS keys, JWTs, and database connection strings are scrubbed before any data reaches the LLM.

🌐

No ingress required

No load balancers, no exposed ports, no public endpoints. The MCP server communicates over stdio via kubectl exec.

📋

Scoped RBAC

A minimal ClusterRole grants read-only access to events, nodes, deployments, HPAs, and replicasets — nothing more.
